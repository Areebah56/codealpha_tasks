EXIT 01 — ABOUT

I ended up specializing in the part of software people look at under pressure — dashboards, live status views, and interfaces that stay legible when the data won't sit still.

My current focus is the web dashboard for IVVSP (Intelligent Vehicle Verification & Surveillance Platform), my Final Year Project — built with React, Vite, Ant Design, and Zustand, with role-based routing across super admin, zone admin, and officer views, plus live visual simulations for verification events. I built it against a mock auth layer so I could move independently while the backend came together in parallel.

Before that, coursework took me through native Android — a multi-screen Java app with bottom navigation and intent-based data passing, and a Firebase-backed patient registration app with real authentication and Firestore.

Outside of coursework, I've also designed and marketed a small handcrafted jewelry concept rooted in South Asian artisan techniques — mostly to keep the "build something people want" muscle exercised alongside the engineering side.
